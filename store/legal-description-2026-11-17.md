## App description (paste as one paragraph)

```
Mittu AI is a tarot reading app for entertainment, for adults (18+) only, in which a talking parrot character picks one of the 22 Major Arcana cards for the user's question (love, career, money, health or general) and gives a short reading in Hinglish, Hindi or English, generated by AI (Google Gemini on Vertex AI) and spoken in a computer-generated voice (Google text-to-speech) with subtitles and optional soft background music; readings are not medical, legal, financial or professional advice, and the app shows helpline numbers for distress-related questions. Sign-in is by mobile number with a one-time SMS code; the user then gives a required name and a required date of birth, which confirms they are 18 or older (under-18s are refused), and may optionally add their time of birth, city of birth (typed by the user, no location lookup) and gender; the app works out the sun sign (rashi) from the date of birth, and the name and birth details are sent with each question to Google Gemini only to personalise the reading; they can be edited in the profile, are kept while the account exists and are deleted with the account. The user can type the question or, optionally, speak it: the microphone is used only after the user taps the mic button and stops when they tap it again or finish speaking, the phone's own speech service turns the speech into text, and no audio is recorded, stored or sent to our servers. Questions and readings are saved in the user's history and can be reported; Mittu's generated voice clips are kept for 7 days and then deleted; readings can be shared through the phone's own share sheet. Access is a short paid trial followed by a single monthly subscription (there is no yearly plan), paid via UPI AutoPay through Razorpay, never through Google Play Billing; GST invoices are available in the app; there is no in-app cancel button, so users cancel the AutoPay mandate in their UPI or bank app or by email, and access continues until the end of the trial or the period already paid for. The app uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK, which collects the device's advertising ID, app installs and opens, app interactions, crash and diagnostic data, and a purchase event when a new subscription starts, for analytics and advertising measurement; the app sends no push notifications and asks for no notification permission, and it does not access location, contacts, call logs, SMS, photos or files, and shows no ads. Servers are in India (Mumbai); Gemini requests use Google's global endpoint, so that processing may take place outside India. Users can request account and data deletion on the delete-account page; requests are acknowledged within 2 working days and completed within 30 days.
```
