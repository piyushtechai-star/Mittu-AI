Delete Your Mittu AI Account & Data

Mittu AI · HouseofTech Innovation Pvt Ltd

Last updated: 12 November 2026

We respect your right to control your data. If you no longer want to use Mittu AI, you can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how.

Before you delete — save the readings you want to keep. Deletion permanently removes your account, your name, and all your questions and readings. Open the App and screenshot any readings you want to keep before requesting deletion. We cannot recover them afterwards.

Uninstalling the App does not delete your account. Mittu AI stores your questions and readings on our servers. To remove your data from our servers, you must actively request account deletion using one of the methods below. Simply uninstalling the App from your phone will not delete your account.

Option 1 — Start from inside the App

Open Mittu AI → Profile → Delete account. This opens this page. There is no delete button inside the App: deletion is carried out by our support team, so please send the email request described below.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Send an email to our support team, quoting the mobile number registered on your Mittu AI account:

Email: support@houseoftech.ai

Subject line: Mittu AI Account Deletion Request

Include in the email:

  • The mobile number registered on your account
  • (Optional) Reason for deletion — helps us improve

We may send an OTP to your registered mobile number to verify that the request is genuinely yours before we proceed.

Email us to request deletion

What gets deleted

When we process your request, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account profile — registered mobile number and name
  • Readings — every question you asked, with its category, card, orientation, reading text, and date
  • Reports you sent about readings (the reason and the reading’s text)
  • Mittu’s voice clips for your readings that are still stored (they are deleted automatically after 7 days in any case)
  • Login sessions — you are signed out on all devices
  • Your Razorpay UPI Autopay mandate is cancelled, so you will not be charged again (payment records and GST invoices are retained for statutory compliance — see below)

Note: Account deletion is permanent. Readings cannot be recovered once deletion is complete. Any readings you already screenshotted or shared stay wherever you saved them — deleting your account does not reach those copies.

What may be retained

In accordance with legal and compliance obligations, the following may be retained:

  • GST invoices and payment records for your subscription payments to us (invoice number, mobile number, the name shown on the invoice, amount, date, and payment ID), required by tax law and for our own accounting and audit compliance (typically up to 7 years)
  • OTP and rate-limit records and server logs, kept for fraud prevention and platform security for a limited period [[TO CONFIRM retention, e.g. 30 days]] and then deleted

App events and the advertising ID already sent to Meta through the Meta SDK are held by Meta under its own policy and cannot be deleted by us. You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. Some preferences (such as mute and skip-animation) are stored only on your phone; they are removed when you uninstall the App.

Active subscription

Deleting your account cancels your Mittu AI UPI Autopay mandate. If you only want to stop payments and keep your account, cancel the subscription instead — from your UPI / bank app, or by emailing support. See the Payment & Cancellation Policy for steps. Deleting your account does not automatically issue a refund — refunds are governed by the Cancellation Policy.

Timeframe

  • We acknowledge your request within 2 working days.
  • Deletion is completed within 30 days of verification (or sooner where required by your local law).
  • You will receive a confirmation once deletion is complete.

Questions

If you have questions about data deletion or your privacy rights, see our Privacy Policy or contact support@houseoftech.ai.
