Payment & Cancellation Policy

Mittu AI · HouseofTech Innovation Pvt Ltd

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Policy explains the trial, limits, pricing, payment processing, automatic renewal, cancellation, and refunds for the Mittu AI subscription offered by HouseofTech Innovation Pvt Ltd.

Mittu AI is a paid service. There is no free tier. After you sign in, tarot readings from Mittu need an active Mittu AI subscription, which starts with a short trial at the price shown in the App and then renews monthly at the price shown in the App.

1. Plans

Mittu AI offers one paid subscription: a short trial, then a monthly plan. Plans are currently monthly only. There is no free tier and there are no one-time in-app purchases. Current prices are always displayed on the in-app paywall at the time of purchase — the in-app paywall is the authoritative source and prevails over the indicative figures below.

PlanIndicative priceBillingWhat it includes
Monthly As shown at the paywall / in the App Recurring — auto-renews every month until cancelled Tarot readings from Mittu in every category (Love, Career, Paisa, Health, and General), spoken in Mittu’s voice with subtitles, subject to a daily reading limit.
Trial As shown at the paywall / in the App One payment for a short trial period shown in the App; converts to the monthly plan at the standard price when the trial ends, unless cancelled first Full access for the trial period.

Plan names, prices, inclusions, and availability may change, and offers may be limited in time. Active subscribers have access to all features while their subscription is active. If you cancel, access continues until the end of your trial or the period you have already paid for.

2. Usage Limits

Your subscription is subject to limits that keep the service reliable for all users. Current limits are shown in the App. They cover:

  • Daily readings — a daily cap on the number of readings (fair use).
  • Fair use — automated, bulk, or commercial-scale use beyond normal individual use may be throttled.
  • We may adjust limits from time to time. If a change materially reduces what an active paid plan includes, we will notify you in advance and you may cancel before your next renewal.
  • Exceeding a limit does not incur an extra charge — readings are simply paused until the limit resets the next day.
  • A subscription is for one person. Sharing account credentials or circumventing limits may result in suspension (see our Terms & Conditions).

3. Payment Methods

  • Payments are processed by RBI-licensed payment gateway partners (currently Razorpay Subscriptions). The active payment gateway may change from time to time without prior notice.
  • Supported method: UPI Autopay.
  • When you start the trial you set up a UPI Autopay mandate. You authorise the payment gateway partner to debit the trial amount then, and the agreed monthly amount on each renewal date until you cancel.
  • HouseofTech does not store your card or UPI credentials — they are held by the payment gateway under its own security standards (PCI-DSS).

4. Currency, Pricing & Taxes

  • Prices are shown in Indian rupees (INR).
  • Prices shown include applicable GST.
  • A GST invoice is created for every payment (the trial and each monthly charge). You can view and download it in the App (Profile → Invoices) or request it from support@houseoftech.ai.

5. Auto-Renewal

  • Subscriptions auto-renew at the end of each billing cycle (monthly; the first monthly charge comes when the trial ends) using the UPI Autopay mandate set up when you start the trial.
  • If a renewal payment fails, the payment gateway may retry per its policy. [[TO CONFIRM: does access continue or pause while a failed renewal is pending?]] If payment cannot be collected, your subscription lapses automatically — readings stop until you subscribe again.
  • When a subscription lapses, your saved readings are not deleted. They remain in your account; readings simply stop being available until you subscribe again.

6. How to Cancel Auto-Renewal

You can cancel auto-renewal at any time. Cancellation stops future charges. Your access continues until the end of your trial or the period you have already paid for. There is no cancel button inside the App.

Option A — From your UPI / Bank App

  • Open your UPI app (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM, or your bank’s app) and search for AutoPay (it may also be listed as Mandates, Autopay & Mandates, or Automatic payments).
  • Find the “HouseofTech / Mittu AI” mandate [[TO CONFIRM: the merchant / mandate name users will see in their UPI app]] → choose Cancel to stop future charges.
  • Once the mandate is cancelled we cannot collect the next renewal. Your access continues until the end of your trial or the period you have already paid for, and then stops.

Option B — Email Support

  • Email support@houseoftech.ai quoting your registered mobile number, with the subject “Mittu AI Cancellation Request”. We will cancel the auto-renewal within 2 working days.

7. Refund Policy

Completed payments are non-refundable except in the cases described below or where required by applicable consumer law.

7.1 Eligible refund cases

  • Duplicate charge — if you were charged twice for the same cycle, the duplicate amount is refunded in full within 5–10 working days.
  • Failed activation — if your payment was charged but your subscription was not activated within 24 hours and our support team cannot resolve it, you receive a full refund.
  • Unauthorised charge — if you can show a charge was made without your consent, report it within 7 days; we will investigate and, if confirmed, refund the disputed amount.
  • Statutory rights — if the consumer law of your country grants a mandatory withdrawal/cooling-off or refund right that applies to digital services, we will honour it. Note that in many jurisdictions (e.g., the EU/UK), the 14-day withdrawal right for digital content lapses once you consent to immediate delivery and the service is performed.

7.2 Non-eligible cases

  • Change of mind, loss of interest, or unused subscription time.
  • Dissatisfaction with the card picked or the reading given — cards are picked at random and Mittu AI provides tarot readings for entertainment purposes only.
  • Dissatisfaction with, or reliance on, AI-generated readings. Readings are generated by AI and may not be accurate or suitable for your specific situation (see our Terms & Conditions).
  • Decisions made about love, career, money, health, or anything else based on a reading — Mittu AI does not provide professional medical, legal, or financial advice.
  • Loss of saved readings if you delete your account or lose access to your registered mobile number.
  • Inability to use the App due to user-side issues such as device incompatibility, connectivity, or storage space.
  • Reaching the daily reading limit, or a limit being adjusted with prior notice.
  • Failure to cancel before an upcoming renewal date.
  • Account suspension or termination for violation of our Terms or usage rules.

7.3 How to request a refund

  • Email support@houseoftech.ai with subject “Mittu AI Refund Request”.
  • Include your registered mobile number, the payment ID or invoice number (shown on your GST invoice in the App, Profile → Invoices), and reason for refund.
  • We acknowledge within 2 working days and resolve eligible refunds within 7–10 working days. Refunds are credited back to the original payment method; some banks may take longer.

8. Trial

  • Mittu AI has no free tier. A short paid trial gives you full access before the monthly plan starts.
  • The trial price, trial length, and the monthly price that follows are shown on the paywall before you confirm. The monthly charge begins at the end of the trial unless you cancel before then. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged the monthly price, and your access continues until the trial ends.
  • Trial and introductory offers are limited to one per user, and we may withdraw them at any time for new purchases.

9. Plan Changes & Restoring Purchases

  • Plans are currently monthly only, so there is no plan switching today. If we add other plans, how switching works will be shown at the paywall.
  • If you reinstall the App or sign in on a new device, sign in with the same mobile-number OTP — your subscription is tied to your account. If the paywall still appears, tap “Already paid?” to re-check your active subscription.

10. Price Changes

  • We may revise plan prices. Existing active subscribers continue at the price they originally agreed to until their next renewal cycle.
  • If pricing changes for your next renewal, you will be notified in advance and may cancel before being charged the new rate.

11. Chargebacks & Disputes

  • If you initiate a chargeback through your bank or card issuer without first contacting us, we may suspend or terminate your account during dispute resolution.
  • Please email support first — most issues are resolved faster directly.

12. Deleting Your Account

Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account or data, and deleting your account does not automatically issue a refund. To remove your data, see the Delete Account page — and please save any readings you want to keep first.
