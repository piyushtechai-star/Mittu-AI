Privacy Policy

Mittu AI · HouseofTech Innovation Pvt Ltd

Last updated: 12 November 2026

HouseofTech Innovation Pvt Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”, or the “Company”) operates the Mittu AI application and related services (the “App”).

Mittu AI is a tarot reading app for entertainment only (“Sirf manoranjan ke liye”). You type a question (optional) and pick a category — Love, Career, Paisa (money), Health, or General. Our servers pick one of the 22 Major Arcana tarot cards at random (upright or reversed), and Mittu, an animated talking parrot, “picks” the card in an animation and speaks a short reading in Hinglish, with subtitles. The readings are written by artificial intelligence (AI). All readings need an active, paid Mittu AI subscription, which starts with a short trial.

Your questions and readings are private. The questions you ask and the readings Mittu gives you are stored securely in your account. We do not sell your personal data or use your questions to train public AI models. How we handle your readings and AI generation is explained in sections 3 and 4 below.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use Mittu AI.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account details — your mobile number, which you enter to receive a 6-digit one-time password (OTP) by SMS, and the OTP itself. We do not use passwords; sign-in is by OTP verification.
  • Your name — required after you pay. Mittu says your name in your readings, and it is printed on your GST invoices.
  • Your readings — for each reading: the question you type (if any), the category you pick, the card and its orientation (upright or reversed), the reading text, and the date.
  • Reports you send — when you tap Report on a reading, the reason you give and the text of that reading.
  • Subscription and billing details for your Mittu AI subscription (status, trial and renewal dates, payment ID and amount) and the GST invoice for each payment. Razorpay’s checkout may also ask for your email address, which Razorpay collects under its own policy.
  • Feedback, support queries, or communications you send to us.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address records and OTP records (for example, how many codes were requested and how many wrong attempts were made), used to limit repeated login attempts and to protect the service from abuse.
  • Server logs created when the App talks to our servers.
  • Through the Meta SDK (see section 6): your device’s advertising ID; app events — app install, app open, and a purchase event only when a new subscription starts — used for analytics and to measure our advertising; and crash and diagnostic data.

C. Permissions the App May Request

  • Mittu AI does not use your microphone, location, contacts, photos, or notifications, and does not ask for these permissions.
  • Razorpay’s checkout may check which UPI apps are installed on your phone, to show you payment options.

D. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not access your contact list or photos, and we never record, receive, or store your voice. The voice you hear in the App is Mittu’s computer-generated voice, not yours.
  • We do not use your device location.
  • We do not collect government IDs or biometric identifiers.
  • We do not store your card or UPI details — payment information is handled directly by our RBI-licensed payment gateway partner (currently Razorpay).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Verify your mobile number by OTP, create your account, and keep you signed in.
  • Create your tarot readings: pick a card at random and generate a short reading for your question and category, using your first name.
  • Create Mittu’s spoken voice for each reading and play it to you.
  • Store your readings in your account.
  • Review the reports you send about readings.
  • Process subscription payments, manage billing for your Mittu AI subscription, and issue GST invoices.
  • Send OTPs by SMS.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorised access, and enforce the daily reading limit and our usage rules.
  • Measure app installs and new subscriptions, for analytics and to measure our advertising, through the Meta SDK (see section 6).
  • Improve App performance, fix bugs, and develop new features (using aggregated or de-identified data wherever possible).

3. Your Questions & Readings

Your readings are yours. We do not sell them, mine them for advertising, train public models on them, or share them with other users.

  • Your questions and readings are stored in your account in our database.
  • Access is tied to your account. Other users of the App cannot see your questions or readings.
  • Our staff do not routinely access the contents of your readings; limited access may occur only to review a reading you have reported, for support you have requested, for security investigation, or where legally required.

4. Mittu & AI Generation

  • Mittu’s readings are generated by AI. Mittu is an animated character, not a human tarot reader. The card is picked at random by our servers. To write the reading, the App sends your question, the category, your first name, and the chosen card to Google’s Gemini models through Vertex AI, with Google Cloud acting as our processor (service provider).
  • Gemini requests use Google’s global endpoint, so this processing may take place outside India. [[TO CONFIRM wording with Ayush]]
  • Under Google Cloud’s terms, your prompts and the replies are not used to train Google’s models. [[TO CONFIRM]]
  • Mittu’s voice: the reading text is sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to create Mittu’s voice. The audio clip (Mittu’s voice, not yours) is stored privately in Google Cloud Storage in India (Mumbai), is played to you through a private link that expires after 1 hour, and is automatically deleted after 7 days.
  • Clearly off-topic questions get a fixed reply that is not generated by AI.
  • AI readings can be wrong or unsuitable. Readings are for entertainment only. They are not reviewed by us before you see them. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, financial, or other professional decisions. See our Terms & Conditions. Every reading has a Report button in the App; please use it if a reading seems wrong or inappropriate.
  • Please do not type highly sensitive personal data (for example health records, government ID numbers, or credentials) into your question if you would not be comfortable with it being processed by a cloud service.
  • If a question mentions self-harm, the App does not give a reading. Instead it shows a support message with Indian helpline numbers (Tele-MANAS 14416 or 1-800-891-4416, and 112 for emergencies). Mittu AI is not an emergency or counselling service.

5. Subscription & Payment Information

  • Mittu AI has no free tier. After you sign in, readings need an active Mittu AI subscription, which starts with a short trial at the price shown in the App and then renews monthly at the price shown in the App. Plans are currently monthly only, and there are no one-time in-app purchases. A daily reading limit (fair use) applies and is shown in the App.
  • Payments are processed by RBI-licensed payment gateway partners (currently Razorpay Subscriptions, using UPI Autopay). The active payment gateway may change from time to time without prior notice.
  • Each payment gateway handles and stores your payment details under its own security standards (PCI-DSS). We never see or store your UPI PIN, UPI details, or card number. Razorpay’s checkout may ask for your email address and may check which UPI apps are installed on your phone, to show you payment options.
  • There is no in-app cancel button. To cancel, either (A) open your UPI / bank app, find AutoPay and cancel the Mittu AI mandate, or (B) email support@houseoftech.ai quoting your registered mobile number. Cancelling stops future charges; access continues until the end of your trial or the period you have already paid for.
  • We retain your subscription status and dates, and a GST invoice for each payment (invoice number, mobile number, the name shown on the invoice, amount, date, and payment ID), for billing, accounting, and tax compliance.
  • Details of the trial, limits, renewal, and refunds are in our Payment & Cancellation Policy.

8. Data Storage & Security

  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Account data, questions, and readings are protected with access controls and industry-standard safeguards, and are accessible only through your authenticated account. Mittu’s voice clips are stored privately and can only be played through a link that expires after 1 hour.
  • Some app preferences (such as mute and skip-animation) are stored only on your device; they are subject to your device’s own security.
  • Sign-in uses OTP verification and a session token stored in your phone’s secure storage (Keychain / Keystore). OTP codes expire after 10 minutes, and we limit the number of codes per hour and the number of wrong attempts. Keep your mobile number and device secure; anyone with access to both may be able to sign in to your account.
  • While we take strong precautions, no system is completely secure. Keep your own copies of any readings you cannot afford to lose.
